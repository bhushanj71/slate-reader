Slate Library reading aloud

A reader that
holds still.

Slate opens a PDF as a book — facing pages, one spread at a time, no scrolling — on a simulated electrophoretic panel. No glow, no colour, and only the two marks a real panel leaves: the refresh flash, and the ghost of the page before. It reads aloud when your eyes are done, and it always remembers the line you stopped on.
